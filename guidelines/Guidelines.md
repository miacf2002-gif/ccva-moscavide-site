# CCVA Moscavide — Design Guidelines

## Aesthetic: Quiet Luxury (Warm & Accessible)

Ivory and warm cream as the page ground, deep charcoal text, warm brass gold as the single accent. Generous negative space, full-bleed photography, high-contrast serif headings paired with a humanist sans for body — designed for all ages.

## Palette
- Background: `#F7F3EE` — warm ivory
- Foreground: `#1A1714` — deep charcoal
- Card: `#FBF8F5`
- Muted: `#EDE7DF`
- Muted foreground: `#6B5F55`
- Accent: `#B8934A` — warm brass gold (used sparingly)
- Border: `#D8CFC6` — warm hairline

## Typography
- **Display / Headings**: Playfair Display (serif) — dignified, warm, accessible at all sizes
- **Body / UI**: Source Sans 3 (humanist sans) — high legibility, large x-height

## Shapes & Radii
- `--radius: 2px` — nearly square, no rounded cards
- No shadows, no gradient backgrounds

## Imagery
- Unsplash warm-toned photography
- Full-bleed with dark overlay gradients
- Always include descriptive alt text
